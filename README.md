# 👋 Hi, I'm Nika

I'm an MSc Bioinformatics student at the University of Birmingham, with a background in Cellular and Molecular Biology.

My interests lie in understanding biological systems through the relationships between their components, and in exploring how computational approaches can help us study complex biological questions.

---

## 🧬 Research Vision

I am interested in synthetic biology and computational systems biology, particularly in how molecular components interact to form networks and influence the behaviour of biological systems.

My developing research vision centres on integrating biological data with systems thinking and computational approaches to investigate disease mechanisms. In the longer term, I hope to contribute to a more integrated understanding of disease and to approaches that support precision medicine.

### Research interests

- **Synthetic biology**
- **Computational systems biology**
- **Biological networks and disease mechanisms**
- **Transcriptomics and multi-omics integration**
- **Cancer biology and precision medicine**

## 🔬 Current Project

### TCGA-BRCA: RNA-seq and Clinical Data Analysis

I am working on a project that explores relationships between breast cancer gene expression and clinical characteristics using TCGA-BRCA data.

The project brings together RNA-seq data analysis, statistical modelling and visualisation in a reproducible workflow.
